The same kitchen chair, four times, over about a week. Not because the chair is
interesting but because it is the only object in the flat that holds still and
has a complicated relationship with the window.

The first is too careful. The second is too loose, in the way that people are
loose when they have been told the first one was too careful. The third is the
only one that is any good, and I do not know why. The fourth is an attempt to
reproduce the third, which is exactly the wrong reason to draw anything.
