Six frames, all within about forty minutes, all from the same twenty metres of
path above the waterline. I had arrived early for someone who, it turned out,
was not coming, and rather than leave I walked up and down the same stretch
until the light went.

The tide was still going out. What I wanted was the moment where the wet sand
stops behaving like ground and starts behaving like sky — a thing you can see
happening but cannot really point at.

> Portra is too forgiving for this. It wants everything to be pleasant. Half of
> these are pleasant in a way I did not mean.

The two that work are the ones where I stopped composing. Which is an annoying
lesson to be handed for the fourth or fifth time.
